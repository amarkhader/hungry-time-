Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We deliver through WhatsApp orders and selected delivery partners. Orders over $25 get free delivery within a 5km radius. Average delivery time is 30-45 minutes.

Can I customize my order?

Absolutely! You can add or remove any ingredient from your order. Just mention your preferences when ordering via WhatsApp or phone. We're happy to accommodate allergies and dietary needs.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes, we offer a Veggie Garden Burger, Grilled Paneer Wrap, and several salads. We can also modify most dishes to be vegan-friendly upon request. Just ask!

How do I reserve a table?

You can reserve a table by clicking the "Reserve Table" button on our website, calling us directly, or sending a WhatsApp message. We recommend booking at least 2 hours in advance for weekends.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. For delivery, cash and card on delivery are both available.

Do you cater for events and parties?

Yes! We offer catering packages for birthdays, corporate events, and private parties. Contact us at least 48 hours in advance with your guest count and preferences for a custom quote.
